# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are owners and managers of Czech companies who need a new business website, a custom web application, an internal system, process automation, or help with software that already exists.

A second audience is product and engineering teams looking for an experienced full-stack contractor who can take ownership of a defined technical area and work inside an existing delivery process.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Bc. Ondrej Halata's work and turns relevant project situations into direct enquiries. It should help visitors quickly understand what Ondrej delivers, see evidence that he has built comparable systems, and choose a sensible next step without needing a finished specification.

## Positioning

Ondrej combines analysis, product and process understanding, full-stack implementation, deployment, and ongoing operation in one accountable technical partner. He can deliver a project independently from zero or join an established team and take ownership of a defined workstream.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive with one of four situations: they need a new website or application, their current system needs takeover or further development, manual work should be automated, or their team needs experienced delivery capacity. The preferred first action is a short description of the situation; a call is available when it is the faster next step.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Custom company websites, web applications, internal systems, administration interfaces, data integrations, automation, and practical AI implementation.
- Takeover, stabilisation, maintenance, and continued development of existing or legacy applications.
- Contract cooperation inside product and software teams, including delivery, architecture, operations, Linux, Docker, and cloud environments.
- The website is a bilingual Czech and English Next.js application with a large indexed content library. Existing public routes, metadata, structured content, and SEO validation must remain intact.
- WordPress, its ecosystem, and PHP-based delivery are not part of the offer.

## Brand Commitments

- The public name is Bc. Ondrej Halata.
- The voice is professional, educational, technically credible, and human. It avoids generic agency hype, exaggerated claims, and formulaic openers.
- The work should be presented through concrete project situations, commercial experience, and real delivered systems rather than abstract technology lists.
- The site must work in Czech and English and remain accessible, responsive, and fast.

## Evidence on Hand

- `halatao.cz`: personal website and commercial entry point.
- `doporucenoai.cz`: an owned GEO and AI visibility analysis application.
- `prodat-byt.cz`: a website for a real-estate sales service.
- `kasanpelcova.cz`: a real-estate company website with content and listing management.
- `crm.halatao.cz`: a demo of a real, fully implemented real-estate CRM application.
- `emamky.cz` and `novinex.cz`: two content magazines built on one shared platform.
- `swapio.cz`: a marketplace and classifieds project.
- `viditelnymakler.cz`: a real-estate focused web project.
- Commercial and contractor experience in larger teams and existing codebases is real, but some work cannot be shown publicly in detail.
- No testimonials, performance benchmarks, or commercial figures should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the business situation and the result, then explain the technology.
2. Show concrete delivered work early enough to establish credibility.
3. Make independent delivery and team-based cooperation equally understandable.
4. Give visitors a clear next step even when their scope is incomplete.
5. Keep claims factual and specific enough to be defensible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must preserve semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
